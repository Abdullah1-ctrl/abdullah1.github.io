How to Create a Website for Beginners

Beginner Guide • 2026 • Abdullah Blog

Creating a website is one of the most valuable digital skills today. Even if you are a complete beginner, you can build a website step by step.

1. Choose Your Website Idea

Pick a niche like blogging, tech, education, or portfolio.

2. Buy a Domain

Your domain is your website name like example.com.

3. Get Hosting

Hosting stores your website files online so people can visit it.

4. Build Website

Use HTML/CSS or WordPress to design your website.

5. Publish Content

Write helpful articles that solve problems.

Final Tip

Consistency is the key to success in blogging.
