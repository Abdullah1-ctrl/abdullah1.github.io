How to Create a Website for Beginners

Creating a website is one of the most important digital skills today. In this guide, you will learn step-by-step how to build your first website even if you are a complete beginner.

1. Choose a Website Idea

Decide what your website will be about: blog, portfolio, tech site, or business website.

2. Get a Domain Name

A domain is your website address like www.yoursite.com. Keep it short and easy to remember.

3. Buy Hosting

Hosting stores your website online so people can visit it anytime.

4. Build Your Website

You can use HTML/CSS or platforms like WordPress. Beginners should start with simple HTML.

5. Add Content

Write helpful articles. Google loves useful content.

6. Publish & Grow

Keep improving your website and publish regularly.

Final Tip

Consistency is the key to success in blogging.
