How to Create a Website for Beginners

Step-by-step guide for beginners

Creating a website may look difficult, but today it is very easy. You just need the right steps and basic knowledge. In this guide, you will learn how to create your first website from scratch.

Step 1: Choose Your Website Idea

First decide what your website will be about. It can be a blog, portfolio, business site, or tech website.

Step 2: Buy a Domain Name

A domain name is your website address like www.example.com. Choose a short and easy name.

Step 3: Get Hosting

Hosting stores your website files and makes them online. Without hosting, your website cannot be seen on the internet.

Step 4: Create Your Website

You can build a website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript or use platforms like WordPress.

Step 5: Add Content

Write helpful articles and useful information for visitors. Good content is very important for Google ranking.

Step 6: Make It SEO Friendly

Use proper headings, keywords, and descriptions so your website appears in Google search results.

Step 7: Apply for Google AdSense

After publishing good content and pages like About, Contact, and Privacy Policy, you can apply for AdSense.

Conclusion

Building a website is easy if you follow the right steps. Start simple, keep learning, and improve your website over time.
