What is Google AdSense?

Monetization Guide • 2026 • Abdullah Blog

Google AdSense is a program that allows website owners to earn money by showing ads on their website.

How It Works

Google places ads on your website. When visitors see or click ads, you earn money.

Requirements

You need: - A website with content - Original articles - Privacy policy page - Good traffic

Approval Tips

✔ Write original content ✔ Avoid copied material ✔ Make clean design ✔ Add important pages

Final Tip

Focus on content first, money comes later.
